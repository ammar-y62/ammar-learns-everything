# Tech Companies Overview

### AWS
**Industry:** Cloud Computing

**What they do:** AWS (Amazon Web Services) provides on-demand cloud infrastructure and services including computing power, storage, and databases.

**Mini Example:** A startup uses AWS Lambda and DynamoDB to run a serverless backend that scales automatically with user traffic.

---

### Google
**Industry:** Technology & AI

**What they do:** Google builds products powered by advanced AI and search technology, ranging from advertising to cloud services and Android OS.

**Mini Example:** A marketing team uses Google Ads and Google Analytics to drive and analyze customer traffic for an e-commerce campaign.

---

### Meta
**Industry:** Social Media & VR

**What they do:** Meta develops platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp while investing heavily in virtual and augmented reality.

**Mini Example:** A content creator uses Instagram Reels to reach new audiences and track engagement using Meta’s Creator Studio.

---

### Microsoft
**Industry:** Software & Cloud

**What they do:** Microsoft provides enterprise software, productivity tools (like Office 365), cloud services (Azure), and developer platforms.

**Mini Example:** A fintech company hosts their APIs on Azure Kubernetes Service and uses Microsoft Teams for internal communication.

---

### Netflix
**Industry:** Streaming & Entertainment

**What they do:** Netflix is a subscription-based streaming platform that produces and distributes digital video content globally.

**Mini Example:** A data team at Netflix uses recommendation algorithms to suggest personalized movies and shows to each user.

---

### Cisco
**Industry:** Networking & Security

**What they do:** Cisco builds hardware and software solutions for networking, cybersecurity, and collaboration in enterprise environments.

**Mini Example:** An IT team deploys Cisco routers and Webex to securely connect remote offices and facilitate global video conferencing.

---

### IBM
**Industry:** Enterprise IT & AI

**What they do:** IBM offers cloud platforms, consulting services, and AI tools like Watson for enterprise digital transformation.

**Mini Example:** A healthcare provider uses IBM Watson to analyze patient data and recommend personalized treatment plans.

---

### Affirm
**Industry:** Fintech & BNPL

**What they do:** Affirm provides buy now, pay later (BNPL) financing solutions for online shoppers and merchants.

**Mini Example:** An online furniture store partners with Affirm to let customers split large purchases into interest-free monthly payments.

---

### Clio
**Industry:** Legal Technology

**What they do:** Clio builds cloud-based software to help law firms manage clients, cases, billing, and documents efficiently.

**Mini Example:** A small law firm uses Clio Manage to organize client case files, schedule court dates, and automatically generate invoices.

---

### Helcim
**Industry:** Fintech & Payments

**What they do:** Helcim provides payment processing and point-of-sale solutions for small businesses with transparent pricing.

**Mini Example:** A local café uses Helcim’s POS system to accept credit card payments and manage daily sales reports.

---

### Benevity
**Industry:** Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Software

**What they do:** Benevity creates software to help companies run donation matching, volunteering, and grant programs for social impact.

**Mini Example:** A tech company uses Benevity to let employees donate to nonprofits and track their volunteer hours.

---

### Arcurve
**Industry:** Software Consulting

**What they do:** Arcurve provides custom software development and digital transformation services to businesses across industries.

**Mini Example:** A utility company partners with Arcurve to modernize its legacy billing system using a cloud-native architecture.

---

### Symend
**Industry:** Customer Engagement & AI

**What they do:** Symend uses behavioral science and AI to help enterprises engage customers who are at risk of default or churn.

**Mini Example:** A telecom company uses Symend’s platform to create personalized outreach for customers late on their bills.

---

### Rokt
**Industry:** E-commerce & Marketing Tech

**What they do:** Rokt delivers targeted offers and upsell experiences at the transaction moment in e-commerce platforms.

**Mini Example:** An online ticketing site uses Rokt to show personalized offers right after a customer completes checkout.

---

### PureWeb
**Industry:** Cloud Streaming & 3D Visualization

**What they do:** PureWeb enables real-time streaming of high-fidelity 3D applications for architecture, automotive, and digital twins.

**Mini Example:** An architect uses PureWeb to stream an interactive 3D model of a building to a client in real time.

---

### Abnormal AI
**Industry:** Cybersecurity & AI

**What they do:** Abnormal AI provides advanced email security by using behavioral AI to detect and stop sophisticated threats.

**Mini Example:** An enterprise deploys Abnormal AI to protect against phishing and business email compromise attacks.

---
